# Evidence scope

- E5 provides three independently trained Transolver3 models and matching CA heads on ShapeNet-Car.
- The present LinearNO accuracy and timing reuse is one training seed, not a three-seed backbone comparison.
- Uniform-normalized rAUEC describes each model's sampling curve; it is not an absolute cross-backbone accuracy rank.
- Existing fixed-prediction reconstruction parity is verified within its recorded cases and tolerance.
- New-model old/new end-to-end accuracy and cost remain pending. No complete-pipeline acceleration claim follows from sparse-map application alone.
- All unfavorable and legacy rows are retained with their protocol labels.
