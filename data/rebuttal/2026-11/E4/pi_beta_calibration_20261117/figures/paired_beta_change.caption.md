For each sampling seed, subtract the beta0.5 rAUEC from the validation-selected beta rAUEC using the same case population, selected-point group and Uniform denominator. Points show the mean of the three differences; error bars show their paired sample SD (ddof=1). Negative change indicates improvement relative to beta0.5. It does not by itself imply that the new rAUEC is below Uniform=1; that is shown separately in test_beta_comparison. There is one frozen training model. No test-based policy choice or significance test is added.
