\subsection{Transformer Neural Operators}
Transformer neural operators use attention to approximate nonlocal integral operators. Let $\Omega$ denote the physical domain, and let $\rho$ denote the sampling measure induced by the chosen grid or mesh discretization policy. A discretized example consists of pointwise samples
$\{(x_i,a_i)\}_{i=1}^{N}\mapsto\{u_i\}_{i=1}^{N}$, where $x_i\sim\rho$, $a_i=a(x_i)$ is the input field, and $u_i=u(x_i)$ is the output field. A neural operator learns an operator $\mathcal{G}:a\mapsto u$ with a parameterized network $\mathcal{G}_{\theta}$.

OFormer~\citep{li2023oformer} uses Galerkin attention, a softmax-free linear-attention factorization that reduces the quadratic point-to-point interaction to linear dependence on $N$ for fixed channel width. After pointwise projections, let $Q,K\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times d}$ and $V\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times C}$, where $d$ is the attention width and $C$ is the number of value channels. Denote the $\ell$-th query and key basis functions by $q_\ell$ and $k_\ell$, and the $c$-th value basis function by $v_c$; their pointwise evaluations are obtained from the embedded samples $(x_i,a_i)$. With column-wise normalized quantities denoted by a hat, Galerkin attention is
\begin{equation}
    y_{i,c}^{(N)}
    = \sum_{\ell=1}^{d} \widehat{q}_{\ell}(x_i)
      \left(\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=1}^{N}
      \widehat{k}_{\ell}(x_n)\widehat{v}_{c}(x_n)\right)
    \xrightarrow[N\to\infty]{}
    \sum_{\ell=1}^{d} q_{\ell}(x_i)
      \int_{\Omega} k_{\ell}(x)v_{c}(x)\,\mathrm{d}\rho(x).
    \label{eq:oformer_galerkin}
\end{equation}
Here $q_\ell$ and $k_\ell$ span the learned query and key bases, while $v_c$ denotes a value channel. Equivalently, the update is $Y=\widehat{Q}(\widehat{K}^{\top}\widehat{V})/N$, which directly approximates the corresponding integral operator.

Transolver~\citep{wu2024transolver} replaces point-to-point attention with Physics-Attention: point features are projected into $M$ slice weights, attention is applied to the resulting latent physical states, and the updated states are broadcast back to the points. To avoid overloading the sample locations $x_i$, let $h_i\in\mathbb{R}^{C}$ denote the embedded point feature obtained from $(x_i,a_i)$. The slice weights are
\begin{equation}
    w_i
    = \operatorname{Softmax}\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{Linear}(h_i)}{\tau_0}\right)
    \in\mathbb{R}^{M},
    \qquad
    \sum_{j=1}^{M}w_{i,j}=1,
    \label{eq:transolver_slice_weights}
\end{equation}
where $\tau_0$ is the temperature. The corresponding physical state is the normalized weighted aggregation
\begin{equation}
    s_j^{(N)}
    = \frac{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}w_{i,j}h_i}
           {\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}w_{i,j}}
    \xrightarrow[N\to\infty]{}
    s_j^{\rho}
    = \frac{\int_{\Omega}w_j(x)h(x)\,\mathrm{d}\rho(x)}
           {\int_{\Omega}w_j(x)\,\mathrm{d}\rho(x)}.
    \label{eq:transolver_physics_state}
\end{equation}

Writing $s=[s_1^{(N)},\ldots,s_M^{(N)}]^{\top}\in\mathbb{R}^{M\times C}$ and $q,k,v=\operatorname{Linear}(s)$, the discrete state attention and its continuous integral counterpart can be written together as
\begin{equation}
    \begin{aligned}
    s_j'
    &= \sum_{t=1}^{M}
      \frac{\exp\!\left(q_j^{\top}k_t/\sqrt{C}\right)}
           {\sum_{p=1}^{M}\exp\!\left(q_j^{\top}k_p/\sqrt{C}\right)}v_t \\
    &\xrightarrow[M\to\infty]{}
    \mathcal{A}[s^{\rho}](\xi_*^s) \\
    &:= \int_{\Omega_s}
      \frac{\exp\!\left(q(s^{\rho}(\xi_*^s))^{\top}k(s^{\rho}(\xi_s))/\sqrt{C}\right)}
           {\int_{\Omega_s}\exp\!\left(q(s^{\rho}(\xi_*^s))^{\top}k(s^{\rho}(\eta_s))/\sqrt{C}\right)\,\mathrm{d}\rho_s(\eta_s)}
      v(s^{\rho}(\xi_s))\,\mathrm{d}\rho_s(\xi_s).
    \end{aligned}
    \label{eq:transolver_state_attention}
\end{equation}
where $s^{\rho}(\xi_s)$ is the continuous state field on the induced slice domain $\Omega_s$ with measure $\rho_s$, and $\xi_*^s$ denotes a latent query location. Finally, the updated states are mapped back to the point space through the slice weights,
\begin{equation}
    h_i' = \sum_{j=1}^{M}w_{i,j}s_j',
    \qquad i=1,\ldots,N.
    \label{eq:transolver_deslice}
\end{equation}
Thus, Physics-Attention replaces the $N\times N$ point interaction with operations of complexity $\mathcal{O}(NMC+M^2C)$ for $M\ll N$. During training, the point sets used by $\mathcal{G}_{\theta}$ are typically generated according to the prescribed measure $\rho$.
