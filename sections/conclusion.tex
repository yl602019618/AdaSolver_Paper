\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we propose AdaSolver, a measure-aware framework for adaptive spatial computation in point-based neural operators. By correcting sampling-induced bias in latent aggregation, AdaSolver supports adaptive point allocation and distribution-amortized training while remaining consistent with the reference operator. Experiments on five standard PDE benchmarks, three industrial aerodynamic prediction tasks, and airfoil and wing optimization demonstrate improved accuracy at matched point budgets and faster surrogate-based design. This study establishes spatial sampling as an axis of test-time scaling for point-based neural operators, offering a direction for more efficient simulation and design in science and engineering.
