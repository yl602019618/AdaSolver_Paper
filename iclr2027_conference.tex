\begin{abstract}
Neural operators can accept irregular point sets, but changing the sampling density can alter the empirical measure used by point aggregation. We study spatial resolution as a test-time compute resource and develop a measure-aware framework for adaptive point allocation with a frozen transformer neural operator.
\end{abstract}
